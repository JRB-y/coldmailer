## 1. Scrub every new list before you mail it

Validate addresses the moment you capture them. Catch typos, throwaway inboxes, and role accounts so you never send to risky contacts in the first place.

## 2. Warm the domain behind your campaigns

Ramp up sending volume gradually, starting with your most engaged contacts. Healthy engagement signals train inbox providers to trust your domain.

## 3. Rotate templates, but keep compliance consistent

Personalize without sacrificing consent. Make opt-out links obvious and log every unsubscribe across the workspace.

## 4. Track results beyond open rates

Monitor bounce codes, spam complaints, and validation outcomes. They reveal issues far sooner than vanity metrics.

## 5. Reload credits before the campaign crunch

Nothing stalls a launch like hitting zero credits mid-upload. Schedule top ups so you always have room for last-minute list refreshes.
